
Nominal MPC has some \textbf{inherent robustness}, especially with
soft constraints, providing Input-to-State Stability (ISS) with
respect to small disturbances in an enlarged feasible set. However,
it lacks guaranteed robust constraint satisfaction and quantitative
stability for larger disturbances. Robust MPC explicitly accounts for
known disturbance/model-error bounds to ensure robustness by design.

\textbf{Uncertain System} (Additive Disturbance Case)
\[
  x(k+1) = A x(k) + B u(k) + w(k), \quad w(k) \in \mathcal{W}
\]

\subsubsection{Inherent Robustness of Nominal MPC}

For linear systems with bounded additive disturbances,
soft-constrained nominal MPC (with slack variables on state
constraints and a contractive terminal set) is ISS in the soft
feasible set $\mathcal{X}_N^\text{soft}$ for sufficiently small disturbances.

Key: Optimal cost $V_N^*$ is continuous and serves as an ISS-Lyapunov
function for small $w$.

Limitations: No guarantees for large disturbances; hard constraints
may be violated.

\subsubsection{Min-Max Robust Formulation}

A direct approach to robustness:
\begin{align*}
  \min_{U} &\max_{W} \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \ell(x_{i}, u_{i}) + \ell_f(x_N)\\
  &  \text{s.t.} \quad x_{i+1} = A x_{i} + B u_{i} + w_{i}, \\
  &  \forall w_{i} \in \mathcal{W}, \quad x_{i} \in \mathcal{X},
  \quad  u_{i} \in \mathcal{U}, \quad x_N \in \mathcal{X}_f
\end{align*}
This is computationally expensive (non-convex, scenario tree explodes).

\subsection{Robust Open-Loop MPC}

Predict open-loop inputs; tighten constraints using disturbance
reachable sets assuming no future feedback.

\ssthl{Disturbance reachable set} \quad $\mathcal{F}_i =
\bigoplus_{j=0}^{i-1} A^j \mathcal{W}$

\begin{sstTitleBox}[RoyalBlue]{Robust Open-Loop MPC}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.55\linewidth}
      \[
        \begin{aligned}
          \min_U \      & \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \ell(x_i, u_i) + \ell_f(x_N) \\
          \text{s.t.}\  & x_{i+1} = Ax_i + Bu_i                   \\
      \end{aligned}\]
    \end{minipage}
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.4\linewidth}
      \[
        \begin{aligned}
          & x_0 = x(k)                                  \\
          & x_i \in \mathcal{X} \ominus \mathcal{F}_i   \\
          & u_i \in \mathcal{U}                         \\
          & x_N \in \mathcal{X}_f \ominus \mathcal{F}_N \\
      \end{aligned}\]
    \end{minipage}
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
\end{sstTitleBox}

Conservative as it ignores future feedback.

\subsection{Closed-Loop Robust MPC (Constraint-Tightening)}

Separate control: nominal $z(k+1)=Az(k)+Bv(k)$ and error compensation
$u_i = v_i + K(x_i - z_i)$, with $K$ stabilizing ($A_K = A+BK$ Schur stable).

Error dynamics: $e_{i+1} = A_K e_i + w_i$, assuming $e_0 = 0$ (since
$z_0 = x(k)$).

Reachable sets: $\mathcal{F}_0 = \{0\}$, $\mathcal{F}_{i+1} = A_K
\mathcal{F}_i \oplus \mathcal{W}$.

\begin{sstTitleBox}[RoyalBlue]{Constraint-Tightening MPC}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.57\linewidth}
      \[
        \begin{aligned}
          \min_{Z,V} \         & \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \ell(z_i, v_i) +
          \ell_f(z_N) \\
          \mathrm{s.t.}\  & z_{i+1} = Az_i + Bv_i                   \\
      \end{aligned}\]
    \end{minipage}
    \begin{minipage}[t]{0.4\linewidth}
      \[
        \begin{aligned}
          & z_0 = x(k)                                       \\
          & z_i \in \mathcal{X} \ominus \mathcal{F}_i        \\
          & v_i \in \mathcal{U} \ominus K\mathcal{F}_i     \\
          & z_N \in \mathcal{X}_f \ominus \mathcal{F}_N \\
      \end{aligned}\]
    \end{minipage}
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \textbf{Control Law}\\
    $u(k) = v_0^\star(x) + K(x(k) - z_0^\star(x)) = v_0^\star(x)$
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \textbf{Properties}
    Recursive feasibility, robust constraint satisfaction,
    ISS w.r.t. $w$ on feasible set.
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
\end{sstTitleBox}

\subsection{Rigid Tube MPC}

Use minimal RPI set $\mathcal{E} = \mathcal{F}_\infty =
\lim_{i\to\infty} \mathcal{F}_i$ (smallest set invariant under error dynamics).

Assume initial error $e_0 \in \mathcal{E}$, optimize $z_0$ s.t. $x
\in z_0 \oplus \mathcal{E}$.

Tube: $x_i \in z_i \oplus \mathcal{E}$.

Tighten uniformly: $z_i \in \mathcal{X} \ominus \mathcal{E}$, $v_i
\in \mathcal{U} \ominus K \mathcal{E}$.

\begin{sstTitleBox}[RoyalBlue]{Rigid Tube MPC}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \[
      \begin{aligned}
        \textbf{Cost} & \quad
        J(Z,V):=\sum_{i=0}^{N-1}
        \ell(z_i,v_i)+\ell_f(z_N)
        \\
        \textbf{Feasible set}  & \quad
        \mathcal{Z}(x):=
        \begin{cases}
          z_{i+1} & = Az_i+Bv_i                         \\
          z_i     & \in \mathcal{X}\ominus\mathcal{E}   \\
          v_i     & \in \mathcal{U}\ominus K\mathcal{E} \\
          z_N     & \in \mathcal{X}_f \ominus \mathcal{E}                   \\
          x     & \in z_0\oplus\mathcal{E}            \\
        \end{cases}
        \\
        \textbf{Control}   & \quad
        \mu_\text{rigid}(x):=
        K(x-z_0^\star(x))+v_0^\star(x)
        \\
        \textbf{Optimization}
    \end{aligned}\]
    \[
      (V^\star(x),Z^\star(x))=
      \argmin_{V,Z}\{J(Z,V)|(Z,V)\in\mathcal{Z}(x)\}
    \]
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \textbf{Properties}
    Robust invariance of feasible set; state converges to $\mathcal{E}$.
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
\end{sstTitleBox}

\subsection{Homothetic Tube MPC}
For state/input-dependent uncertainty: $w(k) \in
\mathcal{W}(x(k),u(k))$, e.g., $w = \Delta A x + \Delta B u + d$,
$(\Delta A, \Delta B) \in \Omega$, $d \in \mathcal{D}$.

Use scaled tubes: $\mathcal{F}_i = \{e \mid V(e) \leq \delta_i\}$,
where $V(e) = \|e\|_M$ is a Lyapunov function (designed offline with
contraction $\rho < 1$).

Predict $\delta = (\delta_0,\dots,\delta_N)$ online.

Tube propagation: $\delta_{i+1} \geq \rho \delta_i + \bar{w}(z_i,
v_i)$, $\bar{w}(z,v) = \max_{w \in \mathcal{W}(z,v)} \|w\|_M$.

\begin{sstTitleBox}[RoyalBlue]{Homothetic Tube MPC}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \[
      \begin{aligned}
        \min_{Z,V,\delta} \         & \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \ell(z_i, v_i)
        + \ell_f(z_N) \\
        \mathrm{s.t.}\  & z_{i+1} = Az_i + Bv_i, \\
        & \delta_{i+1} \geq \rho \delta_i + \bar{w}(z_i, v_i), \\
        & z_i \in \mathcal{X} \ominus \mathcal{F}_i(\delta_i), \\
        & v_i \in \mathcal{U} \ominus K \mathcal{F}_i(\delta_i), \\
        & (z_N, \delta_N) \in \mathcal{X}_f, \\
        & V(x - z_0) \leq \delta_0.
    \end{aligned}\]
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
  \begin{sstOnlyFrame}
    \textbf{Control Law}\quad $u(k) = v_0^\star(x) + K(x(k) - z_0^\star(x))$
  \end{sstOnlyFrame}
\end{sstTitleBox}

Offline: Solve LMI for $M, K$ ensuring $V$ decrease under uncertainty.

Constraints often SOCP via Cauchy-Schwarz.

Properties: Less conservative; quantitative convergence to small RPI
set $\{x \mid \|x\|_M \leq \max_{d\in\mathcal{D}} \|d\|_M / (1-\rho)\}$.

\subsubsection{Comparison}
Constraint-tightening: Fixed tubes, conservative for dependent uncertainty.
Homothetic: Predicts tube sizes, reduces conservatism, higher computation.

\subsubsection{Offline Design: LMIs \& Schur Complement}

To find $M$ and $K$ such that $\tilde{A}_K^T M \tilde{A}_K \le \rho^2
M$ for all $\tilde{A}_K \in \text{vertex}(\Omega)$:
1. \textbf{Schur Complement:} $
\begin{bmatrix} \rho^2 M & \tilde{A}_K^T M \\ M \tilde{A}_K & M
\end{bmatrix} \succeq 0$.
2. \textbf{Change of Vars:} Set $E = M^{-1}$ and $Y = KE$ to get
linear matrix inequalities (LMI):
\[
  \begin{bmatrix} \rho^2 E & (AE + \Delta A_i E + BY)^T \\ AE +
    \Delta A_i E + BY & E
  \end{bmatrix} \succeq 0
\]

\subsubsection{Constraint Tightening (Ellipsoidal)}

For a state constraint $a_j^T x \le b_j$, the tightened version is:
$a_j^T z \le b_j - c_{x,j} \delta$ with $c_{x,j} = \|E^{1/2} a_j\|_2$.

\subsection{Robust Stability (ISS)}

A system is \textbf{ISS} in $\Gamma$ if there exist functions $\beta \in
\mathcal{KL}$ and $\gamma \in \mathcal{K}$ such that:
$\|x(k)\| \le \beta(\|x(0)\|, k) + \gamma(\|w\|_\infty)$.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Soft Constraints} Achieve ISS by relaxing $\mathcal{X}$
    with slacks $\epsilon$.
  \item \textbf{Tube MPC} Achieves ISS; state $x$ converges to the RPI
    set $\mathcal{E}$.
  \item \textbf{Comparison} Fixed tubes (offline $\mathcal{F}_i$) are
    simpler; homothetic tubes (online $\delta_i$) are less
    conservative but require SOCP/LMI solvers.
\end{itemize}

\begin{sstOnlyFrame}
  \textbf{Implementation Checklist:}
  \begin{itemize}
    \item [1] Design $K$ (LQR) and $M$ (Lyapunov) offline via LMIs.
    \item [2] Pre-compute tightenings $c_{x,j}, c_{u,j}$.
    \item [3] Solve QP (Fixed) or SOCP (Homothetic) online.
    \item [4] Apply $u = v_0 + K(x-z_0)$.
  \end{itemize}
\end{sstOnlyFrame}
