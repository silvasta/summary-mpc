## Stochastic MPC - Motivation

- Before: Robust MPC
  - Assume bound on worst-case model mismatch
  - Design a robust MPC that accounts for worst-case error
  - Guarantees constraint satisfaction and convergence,
    for any possible disturbance realization

- What is missing
  - Robust designs can be conservative
  - Cannot account for rare extreme events

### Goal

- Develop MPC schemes that account for **random noise**,
  including extreme but rare events
- Exploit distribution of noise in the design

### Learning Objectives

- Understand constrained stochastic optimal control problems
  - Desired properties
  - Deterministic reformulation

- Understand stability concepts in stochastic model predictive control
  - Asymptotic average performance as a typical criterion
  - Derive asymptotic average performance bounds for stochastic MPC
